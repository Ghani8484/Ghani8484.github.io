Sales Account Deduplication Pipeline

Synthetic CRM data, 708 accounts

  • Finds duplicate sales accounts across Franchise, Partner and Contractor segments: name variants, cross-segment copies and parent-child links.
  • Python with pandas and RapidFuzz. Blocking on name prefixes keeps comparisons down, token-sort fuzzy scoring ranks candidates, and rules label each match type. Parent-child pairs are linked from relationship data, not merged on name similarity.
  • Result on the test data: 132 candidate pairs, 234 accounts flagged, 33.1% duplicate rate.
  • Accounts loaded into Salesforce and staged in Snowflake for SQL profiling. Tableau dashboard shows KPIs, match types, segment split and the flagged pairs. The dataset is synthetic and modeled on typical CRM mess, so this demonstrates the workflow, not a production deployment.
